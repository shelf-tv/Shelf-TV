# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Apple TV–style home screen: a Top Shelf that takes its colours from the focused app, a row of favourites, and every
  other app in a grid below.
- Hold OK on an app to open, move, hide, see App Info or uninstall it.
- Back or Home jumps straight to the top.
- Optional Home Button service, which sends the remote's Home button to Shelf.
- Built for slow streaming sticks: nothing redraws while the screen is still.
