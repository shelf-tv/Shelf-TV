# Changelog

## 1.1.1

- Fixed: holding OK on an app opened it as soon as you let go, instead of leaving its options open to choose from.
  The options now stay up until you pick one.

## 1.1.0

- Shelf now updates itself. When a new version is out, a pop-up on the home screen offers it: choose Update now and
  it installs in moments. Your layout and hidden apps are kept.
- The first time, Android asks you to allow Shelf to install updates (one switch). After that, updates install with
  one click.
- Choose Later and a blue dot on the Settings tile reminds you. Hold OK on Settings to update, or to check for updates.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Apple TV–style home screen: a Top Shelf that takes its colours from the focused app, a row of favourites, and every
  other app in a grid below.
- Hold OK on an app to open, move, hide, see App Info or uninstall it.
- Back or Home jumps straight to the top.
- Optional Home Button service, which sends the remote's Home button to Shelf.
- Built for slow streaming sticks: nothing redraws while the screen is still.
